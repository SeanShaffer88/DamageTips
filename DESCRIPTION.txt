DamageTips
==========

One-line description
--------------------
DPS, DPM, healing output and mana cost % on your action bar and spellbook spell tooltips.

Long description
----------------
DamageTips adds the numbers you actually compare spells with to the tooltips
you already hover over. Point at a spell on your action bar or in your
spellbook and see:

  - DPS: average damage per second, based on cast time, the spell's global
    cooldown, its cooldown and damage-over-time duration
  - DPM: damage per minute
  - Damage per mana: how much damage each point of mana buys (optional)
  - Mana cost: as a percentage of your max mana, an amount, or both
  - Mana sustain: affordable casts and estimated output before low mana (optional)
  - Caster spell stats: spell crit, school spell power and bonus healing (optional)

Spells with a damage range use the average; spells with a direct hit plus a
damage-over-time effect count both (the DoT can be excluded in settings).

Customisable: choose which lines show and where, decimals, thousands
separators, colours, font size and outline - with a live preview. Open it with
/damagetips or from the minimap addon list.

Built for World of Warcraft: Forever (Interface 16001). Follows the
client's secret-value rules, requests spell data it has not loaded yet, and
validates its saved settings. English client only for now.
